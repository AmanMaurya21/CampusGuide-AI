NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — CAREER DEVELOPMENT AND PLACEMENT POLICY
All rules are fictional and intended for demonstration.

1. Career Development Cell
The Career Development Cell (CDC) coordinates campus placement activities, career counseling, resume support, mock interviews and employer communication.

2. Standard eligibility
Students must normally have a cumulative GPA of at least 6.5 and no active backlogs to participate in standard campus placement drives. This is a baseline eligibility rule, not a guarantee of selection.

3. Company-specific criteria
Recruiting companies may impose additional criteria, including a higher GPA, specific degree branches, skills, certifications, project experience or other requirements. Company criteria take precedence for that particular drive where applicable.

4. Registration
Students must register with the CDC before participating in campus recruitment activities. Registration includes maintaining an updated profile, resume and contact details in the placement portal.

5. Participation
Students should attend pre-placement talks, tests, interviews and scheduled recruitment events as instructed. Students are expected to communicate professionally and follow employer instructions.

6. Offers
Receiving an offer does not imply that other recruitment processes will automatically remain available. Students should read the placement participation guidelines and offer-specific conditions.

7. Preparation support
The CDC may offer resume reviews, aptitude practice, mock interviews, technical preparation sessions and career counseling. Participation in these services does not guarantee a job.

8. Eligibility examples
A student with a GPA of 7.0 and no active backlogs ordinarily meets the baseline criteria, subject to company rules. A student with a GPA of 6.3 does not meet the standard baseline. A student with 6.8 GPA but an active backlog also does not meet the standard baseline.

9. Questions
Eligibility and registration queries should be directed to the CDC. Students should include their program, GPA and backlog status when seeking clarification.
