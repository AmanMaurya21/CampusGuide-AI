NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — SCHOLARSHIP AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT POLICY
Fictional policy for demonstration use only.

1. Scholarship categories
The university demonstration includes merit-based awards, need-based support and special-category scholarships. Availability, amounts and conditions may vary by award.

2. Merit scholarship
A merit scholarship may be considered for students with strong academic performance. The sample eligibility threshold for renewal is a semester GPA of at least 8.0 and no active backlogs, unless the award notice specifies different conditions.

3. Need-based support
Need-based support may require income documents, proof of household circumstances and a completed application. Applications are assessed according to the criteria stated in the relevant scholarship notice.

4. Application documents
Applicants may be asked to submit the application form, academic result, identification, bank details and supporting documents. The Scholarship Office may request additional documents for verification.

5. Renewal
Scholarships are not automatically renewed. Students must submit a renewal application by the published deadline and satisfy the criteria applicable to their award. The sample merit-renewal requirement is GPA 8.0 or higher with no active backlogs.

6. Deadlines
Application and renewal deadlines are published by the Scholarship Office. Late applications may not be accepted unless an official exception is announced.

7. Disbursement
Approved awards are disbursed according to the university's published process. The amount and timing depend on the specific award and verification.

8. Appeals and queries
Students may submit a written query or appeal to the Scholarship Office within the deadline specified in the decision notice. Students should keep copies of submitted documents and acknowledgment receipts.
