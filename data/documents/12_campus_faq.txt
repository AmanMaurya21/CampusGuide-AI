NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — STUDENT FAQ
Fictional demonstration FAQ.

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: The ordinary attendance requirement is 75% in each course. Students between 65% and 74% may request exceptional condonation, but approval is not automatic.

Q: How many books can an undergraduate borrow?
A: Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for a standard period of 14 days.

Q: What is the library late fine?
A: The demonstration policy charges ₹5 per book per day for overdue books.

Q: What is the standard placement GPA requirement?
A: The baseline is a cumulative GPA of 6.5 with no active backlogs. Companies may set additional requirements.

Q: What are the sample merit scholarship renewal criteria?
A: A semester GPA of at least 8.0 and no active backlogs, subject to the specific scholarship notice.

Q: Where can I report Wi-Fi problems?
A: Contact the IT Helpdesk in the Academic Block or submit a support request through the university IT portal.

Q: When is the Student Administration Office open?
A: Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, except university-designated holidays or special notices.

Q: How do I request a re-evaluation?
A: Submit an application within seven calendar days of result publication, following the Examination Office's process.

Q: Can medical leave automatically count as attendance?
A: No. Medical documentation may be considered, but it does not automatically convert missed classes into attended sessions or guarantee condonation.

Q: Where can I find my exam timetable?
A: Timetables are published through the student portal and notice boards.

Q: Who can help with course registration?
A: Contact your academic advisor or Program Office. Formal exceptions may require approval from the relevant administration.

Q: Does meeting placement eligibility guarantee a job?
A: No. Eligibility allows participation subject to company requirements; it does not guarantee selection or employment.

Q: Where do I report a lost item?
A: Report it to Campus Security or the Lost and Found desk.

Q: What should I do if a fee payment is not reflected?
A: Submit the transaction reference and receipt to the Student Finance Office and avoid duplicate payment until the original transaction is checked.
