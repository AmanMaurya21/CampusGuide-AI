NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — ATTENDANCE POLICY
Demo institution: All rules in this file are fictional and created solely for the CampusGuide AI demonstration.

1. Minimum attendance
Undergraduate students must attend at least 75% of scheduled teaching sessions in each enrolled course to be ordinarily eligible for the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated course by course, not as a combined semester average. A strong attendance record in one course does not compensate for attendance below the requirement in another.

2. Calculation
Attendance percentage is calculated as sessions attended divided by total sessions conducted, multiplied by 100. For example, attending 36 of 48 sessions gives 75%. The system uses sessions recorded by the course instructor. Students should review attendance records regularly.

3. Attendance correction
A student who believes an attendance entry is incorrect should contact the course instructor within seven calendar days of the entry or publication of the record, whichever is later. The student should provide relevant evidence, such as a timetable or instructor confirmation. Corrections are subject to verification.

4. Condonation
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may request condonation in exceptional circumstances. Condonation is not automatic. Requests are reviewed by the Academic Administration Office and may require supporting documents. Students below 65% are ordinarily not eligible for condonation under this demonstration policy.

5. Medical circumstances
Medical documents may be submitted for consideration when illness has affected attendance. Medical documentation does not automatically convert missed classes into attended sessions or guarantee condonation. Students should submit documentation promptly.

6. Responsibilities
Course faculty maintain attendance records. Students are responsible for checking their records and communicating discrepancies. The Academic Administration Office communicates approved exceptions.

7. Example scenarios
A student with 82% attendance ordinarily meets the attendance threshold, subject to other examination requirements. A student with 70% may apply for condonation but should not assume approval. A student with 61% is ordinarily below the condonation range.

8. Contact
Attendance questions should first be directed to the course instructor. Requests involving exceptions or formal condonation should be directed to the Academic Administration Office.
