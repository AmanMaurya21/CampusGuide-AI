NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — CENTRAL LIBRARY POLICY
Fictional demonstration policy.

1. Access
The Central Library is available to registered students, faculty and staff. Users must carry their university identification card and follow library conduct rules.

2. Borrowing
Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books simultaneously. The standard loan period is 14 days. Books must be returned by the due date. Borrowing privileges may be temporarily restricted when overdue items or unresolved penalties exist.

3. Renewal and reservation
A book may be renewed once for an additional 14 days if no other user has placed a reservation and the renewal is completed before restrictions apply. Students may reserve a currently borrowed book through the library desk or approved library system.

4. Late fees
A late fine of ₹5 per book per day is charged for overdue books under this demonstration policy. Users should check their account before returning items to confirm any outstanding charges.

5. Non-circulating materials
Reference books, journals and newspapers are for use inside the library and are not available for regular borrowing.

6. Opening hours
The library is open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and university-designated holidays. Hours may be adjusted during examination periods through an official notice.

7. Conduct
Users must maintain silence, avoid food and drink in designated areas, and keep shared spaces clean. Damage to books or library equipment may be referred for review.

8. Help
Borrowing, renewal, reservation and account questions should be directed to the library desk. Students should provide their university ID when requesting assistance.
