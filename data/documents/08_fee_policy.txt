NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — STUDENT FEE POLICY
Fictional demonstration policy.

1. Fee categories
Student charges may include tuition, registration, examination, library, laboratory, accommodation and other notified charges. The applicable amount depends on the program and student's status.

2. Payment schedule
Semester fees are ordinarily payable before the semester registration deadline. The Student Finance Office publishes payment dates and instructions.

3. Late payment
A late-payment charge may apply when fees are not paid by the announced deadline. The applicable amount and any grace period are stated in the fee notice.

4. Payment methods
Students may pay through approved online payment methods or other channels announced by the Student Finance Office. Students should retain transaction receipts and payment confirmation.

5. Payment verification
If a payment is debited but not reflected in the student portal, the student should submit the transaction reference and receipt to the Finance Office. Duplicate payments should not be made without checking the status of the original transaction.

6. Refunds
Refund requests are processed according to the refund schedule and conditions published for the relevant academic term. Refund amounts may depend on the request date and applicable rules.

7. Financial holds
Outstanding charges may affect certain administrative services where permitted by university policy. Students should contact the Finance Office to resolve billing questions.

8. Support
Fee-related queries should include the student's ID, semester, charge description and payment evidence.
