NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — EXAMINATION POLICY
All institutional rules and values here are fictional demonstration data.

1. Assessment structure
A standard course is assessed out of 100 marks. Internal assessment normally contributes 40 marks and the end-semester examination contributes 60 marks. Course instructors may communicate the exact internal assessment components at the beginning of the semester.

2. Passing requirements
Students must achieve at least 40 marks out of 100 overall and at least 25% of the end-semester examination component. With a 60-mark examination, the examination-component minimum is 15 marks. Meeting only one of these requirements is insufficient.

3. Internal assessment
Internal marks may include quizzes, assignments, laboratory work, presentations, attendance-related academic activities where permitted, and mid-semester tests. The course instructor should publish the assessment breakdown and submission deadlines.

4. Examination eligibility
Students must satisfy the attendance requirement and any other notified academic requirements before appearing for the end-semester examination. Eligibility is checked separately for each course.

5. Examination-day requirements
Students must carry a university identification card and examination admit card. Students should arrive at least 20 minutes before the scheduled start. Mobile phones, smartwatches and unauthorized electronic devices are prohibited in the examination hall. Permitted materials are specified by the examination notice.

6. Misconduct
Unauthorized communication, copying, impersonation, possession of prohibited material or attempts to disrupt an examination may be treated as misconduct. Consequences may include cancellation of the paper or further disciplinary action depending on the case.

7. Re-evaluation
Students may submit a re-evaluation application within seven calendar days of result publication. Re-evaluation procedures, fees if any, and eligible papers are announced by the Examination Office. Re-evaluation does not guarantee a change in marks.

8. Results and queries
Result-related queries should be submitted through the Examination Office's designated process. Students should retain their registration number and course details when filing a query.
