NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL POLICY
This is fictional demonstration data.

1. Residence allocation
Hostel accommodation is allocated subject to availability, eligibility and the residential office's published process. Allocation is not guaranteed for every student.

2. Entry hours
Residents should return to the hostel by 10:00 PM on regular academic days. Late entry may require recording the reason with the hostel security desk. Special schedules may be announced during university events or examination periods.

3. Leave
Residents planning to stay away overnight should submit a leave request through the residential portal or hostel office. Approval should be obtained before departure except in emergencies. Students should provide an expected return date and emergency contact information.

4. Visitors
Visitors must register at the hostel reception, present identification if requested and follow visitor hours. Visitors are not permitted to enter resident rooms without authorization. Overnight visitor stays are prohibited unless expressly approved under a special procedure.

5. Facilities
Hostels may provide common study rooms, drinking water, laundry facilities and shared recreation areas. Facility availability varies by building and is communicated by the hostel administration.

6. Safety
Residents must not tamper with electrical equipment, fire safety equipment or access controls. Cooking with unauthorized appliances may be prohibited. Emergency exits must remain unobstructed.

7. Complaints
Maintenance, cleanliness and roommate concerns should be submitted to the hostel office. Emergency safety concerns should be reported immediately to hostel security.

8. Discipline
Residents must respect quiet hours, shared spaces and other residents. Repeated violations may be referred to the Residential Administration Office.
